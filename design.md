# Noor Nursery WEBSITE DESIGN BRIEF

## Noor Nursery – Modern, Professional & High-Impact Website

### Project Objective

The objective is to design and develop a *premium, modern, visually impressive website* for Noor Nursery.

The website should immediately create a strong first impression. When a visitor lands on the website, they should feel that Noor Nursery is a *professional, established, trustworthy, and premium brand*.

The design should not look like a basic template or a typical nursery website.

The website should feel like a *premium digital brand experience*.

---

# 1. PRIMARY GOAL

## Create a Strong First Impression

When a visitor opens the website, the first few seconds are extremely important.

The website should communicate:

* Professionalism
* Trust
* Premium quality
* Modern design
* Attention to detail
* Love for nature

The visitor should immediately understand:

> *“This is not an ordinary nursery. This is a premium brand.”*

---

# 2. DESIGN DIRECTION

## Modern + Premium + Natural + Minimal

The website should combine modern web design with the beauty of nature.

The design should be:

* Clean
* Elegant
* Spacious
* Visually rich
* Minimal
* Interactive
* Premium

Avoid:

* Cheap-looking design
* Excessive colors
* Too much text
* Cluttered sections
* Generic templates
* Overuse of animations
* Excessive icons

Every section should have a clear purpose.

---

# 3. FIRST SCREEN / HERO SECTION

The hero section is the most important part of the website.

It should immediately capture attention.

## Recommended Concept

Use a full-screen premium visual experience.

A beautiful high-quality plant or garden scene should dominate the screen.

The design should have depth and a cinematic appearance.

### Main Headline:

# Bring Nature Into Your Life.

### Supporting Text:

Discover beautiful, healthy plants carefully selected for your home, garden, and workspace.

### Call-to-Action:

*Explore Our Collection*

*Discover Your Perfect Plant*

---

## Hero Animation

The animation should feel natural and sophisticated.

When the website loads:

1. The background gradually appears.
2. Plant visuals smoothly reveal.
3. Small natural elements move subtly.
4. The headline appears elegantly.
5. Supporting text fades in.
6. Buttons appear smoothly.

The animation should be subtle.

No excessive movement.

The objective is to create a *premium cinematic experience*.

---

# 4. VISUAL IMPACT

The website should use high-quality visuals.

Images are extremely important for this project.

Use:

* Professional plant photography
* Premium garden photography
* High-resolution images
* Consistent image style
* Natural lighting

Avoid:

* Low-quality images
* Random stock images
* Inconsistent photography
* Too many different image styles

The visual identity should feel consistent across the entire website.

---

Deep Forest Green

Used for:

* Navigation
* Headings
* Important sections
* Branding

### Secondary Color

Used for:

* Buttons

### Accent

---

# 6. TYPOGRAPHY

Typography should feel premium and modern.

Use a combination of:

### Display Font

For large headings.

Elegant and memorable.

### Sans-Serif Font

For:

* Product information
* Navigation
* Descriptions
* Buttons

Typography should have:

* Good spacing
* Strong hierarchy
* Excellent readability

Avoid using too many different fonts.

Maximum:

*Two font families.*

### Product Interaction

The transition should feel smooth.

# 12. TRUST SECTION

Trust is extremely important for an online business.

Include genuine information such as:

* Real customer reviews
* Real customer photographs
* Actual business information
* Social media presence
* Contact information

Only display:

* Real statistics
* Genuine testimonials
* Accurate information

Avoid fake reviews or exaggerated claims.
